## What OCSiAl does

Founded in Luxembourg in 2010, OCSiAl industrialised the synthesis of single wall carbon nanotubes
with its Graphetron reactor line, taking annual capacity from 1 tonne in 2013 toward hundred-tonne
scale, with production and dispersion facilities in Luxembourg and Serbia and laboratories and
TUBALL CENTERs across Europe and Asia. TUBALL was the first single wall carbon nanotube registered
under EU REACH and covered by a US EPA consent order.

## Products

- TUBALL — single wall carbon nanotube (graphene nanotube) raw material.
- TUBALL MATRIX — nanotube concentrates for elastomers, thermoplastics and thermosets.
- TUBALL BATT — nanotube dispersions for lithium-ion battery cathodes and anodes.

Markets served: lithium-ion batteries, elastomers and tires, thermoplastics and composites,
coatings and paints, ESD flooring, silicones, and transparent conductive films.
